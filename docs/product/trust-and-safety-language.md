# Trust & Safety Language

HerStop is **decision support, not a safety guarantee.** This governs all UI copy, docs, and labels.
The enforceable checklist lives in `.claude/rules/product-boundaries.md`; this is the product rationale.

## Why this matters
Claiming a place is "safe" is a promise the app cannot keep and creates real risk and liability.
HerStop builds trust through transparency, review integrity, confidence scoring, and careful language —
not by asserting verdicts.

## Never say (as an absolute claim about a place)
safe · safe stop · verified safe · guaranteed safe · crime-free · objectively safe ·
"the app ensures/guarantees safety".

## Prefer
Recommended Stop · Good Option · Use Caution · Limited Data ·
higher-confidence · better-reviewed · recent traveler feedback · area safety context · confidence score.

## Confidence & labels
- Low review count → **Limited Data**, never an implied verdict.
- Describe what travelers reported ("recent travelers reported good lighting") + how confident the
  data is. Don't collapse that into "safe".

## Reviews
Structured, not just free text: overall feeling, lighting, restroom quality, cleanliness, staff
kindness, loitering/strange-vibe, time of visit, would-stop-again, optional note. Designed with
moderation, reporting, abuse prevention, and privacy in mind.
